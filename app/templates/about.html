Data Integrity Classification

Every figure displayed across this platform is tagged into one of four transparent categories:

VERIFIED — Grounded in official Tata Power documents, PIN directories, and physical hub addresses.
ESTIMATED — Session charge duration and billing previews calculated from rated kW and sample tariffs.
DEMO — Slot reservations, charging simulation meters, and mock ledger settlements (no real bank charges).
UNAVAILABLE — Real-time public bay hardware occupancy when proprietary OCPP API feeds are external.
